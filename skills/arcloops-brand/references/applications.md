# Applications

Quiet / accent / signature levels apply everywhere (see SKILL.md). The full gradient loop runs large in at most one place per piece; accent colors can appear throughout where they carry meaning.

- **Business card**: front is quiet (white, full-color lockup `-01`, contact details). Back is the signature moment: loop bleeds off a corner against charcoal, lockup `-02` or `-03`. Vector files, matte stock, spot gloss on the loop only.
- **Letterhead and envelope**: white paper, charcoal envelope. Loop appears only as a faded corner texture (6 to 10%). Never a loud accent.
- **LinkedIn covers**: canvas 1584 x 396 px. Keep key content upper-left or centered; the profile photo covers the bottom-left corner. Personal banner: "Building Arcloops / AI Advisory, Research, Consulting". Company banner: lockup plus "Advisory, Research, Consulting".
- **Slides**: 16:9, white or charcoal grounds. Geist titles, Inter body. Color loop on cover and close only; section dividers may use the cropped faint loop texture.
- **Documents and web**: white base, charcoal text, hairline dividers, #F7F7F7 bands. No cards with shadows, no colored buttons.
- **Charts**: series order cyan, magenta, amber, charcoal, gray. Label directly and vary line style or markers so color is not the only cue. Axes and grid in #D9D9D9.
- **Web, dashboards, mobile**: see `ui-patterns.md` and the starters in `../templates/`.
