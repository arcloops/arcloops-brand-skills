# Voice and tone

Precise. Grounded. Never hype.

- **Direct**: say what the work is and what it changes. No warm-up sentences.
- **Specific**: name the industry, the process, the number. "Cut month-end close from 9 days to 4" beats "streamline finance".
- **Calm authority**: confident because it is grounded in delivery. No exclamation marks, no superlatives.

Vocabulary
- Use: advisory, research, consulting, audit, consult, train, build, production, workflow, readiness.
- Avoid: revolutionize, cutting-edge, transformative, unlock, game-changing, supercharge, seamless, next-gen.

Positioning line used in the guidelines: "Advisory, Research, Consulting" (the ARC acronym: Advisory sets AI-readiness strategy; Research grounds recommendations in the client's industry; Consulting trains teams and builds the systems). Process: audit, consult, train, build.

Rewrite examples
- Before: "We unlock transformative AI for your business."
- After: "We audit your workflows, train your team, and build the AI systems that fit them."
