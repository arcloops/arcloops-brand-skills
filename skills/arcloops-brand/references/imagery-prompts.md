# Generated imagery (Cursor, fal.ai, any image tool)

Anchor every prompt with these three rules verbatim, then add the specific asset request.

1. **Line, not shape**: thin concentric arcs and orbital linework. Never solid filled icons or blocky geometry.
2. **Three colors, one gradient**: cyan (#06DBF4) to magenta (#E214B1) to amber (#FCB53F), always in that order. The gradient belongs to the loop motif; flat color-blocked fields in the three brand colors are also on-brand for social and web.
3. **Quiet or signature, no middle**: a neutral charcoal/white/gray base, OR one full-saturation gradient hero moment. Never a partially tinted, muddy in-between.

Also: neutral or flat brand-color field, one hero loop at most, no illustration, no photography direction defined yet (ask the user).
